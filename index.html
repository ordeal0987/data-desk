What I do

Data entry

Typing and transferring records from PDFs, scanned documents, web forms and screenshots into Excel or Google Sheets, with consistent formatting.

Excel cleanup

Removing duplicates, flagging or filling blanks, normalising dates, phone numbers and capitalisation, splitting columns, and rebuilding a sheet that is safe to use as a source of truth.

PDF to structured Excel

Statements, catalogues, invoices, student records and reports converted into tidy tables — one row per record, one column per field.

Web research & extraction

Contact lists, competitor tables, product catalogues and directory research, delivered as CSV with the source URL of each record.

Analysis summaries

Descriptive statistics and trend charts (PNG) from a raw dataset, plus a short written summary of what the numbers show.

Proofreading & formatting

Grammar, spelling and punctuation fixes on business documents, plus consistent styling and layout in Word or Google Docs.

How a job runs

  1. You send the files (or links) and describe the output format you need.
  2. I confirm the exact column layout, naming rules and edge cases before starting.
  3. I produce the file and, for large batches, send the first rows for your approval.
  4. You get the finished .xlsx / .csv / .pdf plus a short note on what was cleaned and how.
